Food Service Comfort

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

In a restaurant, hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort are all connected, and an undersized or failing system shows up first as a sweltering cook line and complaints at the tables. Cooking lines dump huge heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air out as fast as the rooftop units can make it. We handle rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Staff can't work a hot line in a kitchen that's pulling negative pressure, and customers notice the drafts at the door and the heat fast. Our service keeps airflow and temperatures where your operation needs them, backed by written reports and service logs after each visit. Those records show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and cover the documentation many commercial leases call for.

Your schedule comes first. We coordinate every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and we keep disruption to a minimum so the kitchen keeps running and the dining room stays comfortable.

Safety

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm proper venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. Findings go into a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Emergency Service

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Dalton

Every hour without climate control costs your business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. On a retail floor, it empties the aisles on a summer afternoon. For a server room, it risks overheated equipment, and in a leased building it invites tenant disputes. Dalton HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Dalton and surrounding areas, dispatching an experienced technician quickly when you call.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: we handle all of it on rooftop and split systems. The priority is diagnosing the fault and getting heating or cooling back fast. Once it's running, we put maintenance and monitoring in place so it doesn't happen again, and each response is fully documented for your facility files.
